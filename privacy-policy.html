Open Shelf Privacy Policy

Last updated: 29 September 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Open Shelf (“we”, “us”, or “our”) handles information when you use the Open Shelf mobile application (“the App”).

1. Information We Collect

Open Shelf is designed to help users discover and read stories and books. Depending on the features used, the App may process information needed to provide its features, information relating to purchases and subscriptions, technical information required for the App to function, and information voluntarily provided when contacting support.

2. In-App Purchases and Subscriptions

Open Shelf may offer paid subscriptions or other in-app purchases. Purchases are processed through the relevant app store, such as Google Play or Apple’s App Store. We do not directly receive or store your complete payment-card details.

Open Shelf may use RevenueCat to help manage subscriptions and determine access to purchased or subscription features. RevenueCat may process purchase and subscription-related information.

For more information, see the RevenueCat Privacy Policy.

3. How We Use Information

  • To provide and operate Open Shelf.
  • To provide access to premium features.
  • To process and verify subscriptions and purchases.
  • To maintain and improve the App.
  • To respond to support requests.
  • To prevent misuse and comply with applicable law.

4. Information Sharing

Information may be processed by service providers needed to operate Open Shelf, including Google Play, Apple’s App Store (if applicable), RevenueCat, and other services that may be added to the App.

We do not sell users’ personal information.

5. Payment Information

Open Shelf does not directly collect or store your full payment-card number. Payments are handled by the relevant app store.

6. Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect information processed through Open Shelf. However, no internet transmission or electronic storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

7. Data Retention

Information is retained only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described in this policy, including providing the App, maintaining subscriptions, resolving disputes, and meeting legal obligations.

8. Children’s Privacy

Open Shelf does not knowingly collect personal information from children in violation of applicable law. If you believe a child has provided personal information improperly, please contact us.

9. Your Privacy Rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights concerning your personal information, including access, correction, deletion, restriction, objection, and portability rights where applicable.

10. Account Deletion

If Open Shelf provides account creation, users will be given a way to request deletion of their account and associated personal information. If the current version does not provide account creation, this section does not apply.

11. Third-Party Services

Open Shelf may use third-party services for payments, subscriptions, hosting, analytics, advertising, or other technical functions. If these services change the information practices of the App, this policy will be updated as necessary.

12. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this policy when Open Shelf’s features or data practices change. The “Last updated” date will be changed when the policy is updated.
